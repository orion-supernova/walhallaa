Saving places

  • Share it: in Instagram, TikTok or Google Maps tap Share → SavorMap.
  • Copy it: copy the link, open SavorMap and tap Paste.
  • Import: Profile → Import brings in Google Maps lists and files from other apps.

A post that names several places pins all of them. If a place lands in the wrong spot, open it and move the pin.

SavorMap Pro

  • New phone? Open the Pro screen and tap Restore purchases.
  • Cancel or change plan: iPhone: Settings → your name → Subscriptions. Android: Play Store → Payments & subscriptions.
  • Refunds are handled by Apple (reportaproblem.apple.com) or Google Play.

Your account

  • Sign in with the same Apple or Google account to see your map on another phone.
  • Delete your account in Profile → Delete account. More about deletion.
