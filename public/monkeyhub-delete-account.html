The fastest way: delete it in the app

If you still have MonkeyHub installed and can sign in, do it from inside the app. It is immediate, it needs no email, and you do not have to wait for us.

  • Open MonkeyHub and sign in.
  • Go to your Profile.
  • Choose Delete Account and confirm.

That is it. Deletion runs straight away and cannot be undone. There is no grace period, no recovery window, and no way for us to bring the account back afterwards.

If you have already uninstalled the app

Email info@walhallaa.com with the subject Delete my MonkeyHub account, and include:

  • Your MonkeyHub username.
  • Enough for us to confirm the account is yours — send the email from the address saved on the account if you added one, or tell us the display name and roughly when you created it.

We verify that the request is genuinely yours before we act on it, because a deletion request from the wrong person is itself a security problem. We aim to complete verified requests within 30 days, and normally much sooner. We will email you when it is done.

Child accounts

A child cannot delete their own MonkeyHub account from their device — the app refuses it. A guardian deletes it from the Family screen in their own MonkeyHub app, and exactly the same erasure runs.

If you are a child and your guardian will not delete your account, write to us at info@walhallaa.com and we will do it. You do not need their permission to ask.

What is deleted

Everything that identifies you as the owner of it, including:

  • Your account record — username, display name, email, password hash, recovery-phrase hash, birth year, account type, privacy preferences, presence.
  • Your profile picture.
  • Every message you sent, and the photos, videos, voice notes and documents attached to them.
  • Your reactions and pinned messages, and records of view-once photos you opened.
  • Your room memberships. A room left empty by your departure is wiped completely, media included.
  • Your friendships and friend requests, in both directions.
  • Blocks you made, and blocks made against you.
  • Guardian relationships, pairing codes and approval requests.
  • Your one-to-one call records and their connection data.
  • Your registered devices and push notification tokens.
  • Your Sign in with Apple and Sign in with Google connections. If you used Sign in with Apple, we also tell Apple to revoke MonkeyHub's access to your Apple ID.

What is kept, and for how long

We would rather tell you this here than have you discover it later. The Privacy Policy has the full detail.

  • Abuse reports — kept 24 months from the date they were filed. Reports you filed have your identity stripped out but are otherwise kept; reports filed about you are kept in full. A service where anyone can erase their own report trail by deleting their account cannot protect its users. After 24 months a scheduled job deletes them.
  • Fragments inside other people's messages. A reply to something you wrote keeps your display name and a short excerpt of it; an @mention keeps your username. Those are part of someone else's message.
  • Group call records — currently not deleted at all. This is a defect in our deletion code, not a policy decision, and we are fixing it. Email us and we will remove them by hand in the meantime.
  • Failed sign-in counters tied to your account, for up to about thirty hours, until a scheduled job clears them.
  • Our push provider's records. We do not yet automatically ask OneSignal to erase your identifier and notification history. Ask us and we will request it manually.
  • Copies on other people's devices. Deletion runs on our servers. It cannot reach a message already delivered to someone else's phone, and it cannot reach the copies on your own device — sign out and uninstall the app for those.
  • Anything the law requires us to keep, including material preserved for a child-safety report.

Deleting the app is not deleting your account

Uninstalling MonkeyHub removes it from your phone. It leaves your account, your messages and your media exactly where they are on our servers. Use one of the two routes above if you want the account itself gone.
