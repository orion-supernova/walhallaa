The fastest way: delete it in the app

  • Open SavorMap and sign in.
  • Go to Profile.
  • Tap Delete account and confirm.

Deletion starts straight away and cannot be undone. You are signed out at once, and your data is erased within minutes.

If you no longer have the app

Email info@walhallaa.com with the subject Delete my SavorMap account, from the email address of the Apple or Google account you signed in with (or tell us which one it was). We verify the request and complete it within 30 days, normally much sooner.

What is deleted

  • Your account and sign-in connection (Apple or Google), name and email.
  • Every save: links, captions, thumbnails and the screenshots you uploaded.
  • Every place on your map, with its notes and tags.
  • Your lists, their cover photos and share links. Places you added to lists owned by friends are removed from those lists.
  • Import history and usage counters.

What is kept

  • Subscription records kept by Apple, Google and RevenueCat, as their policies and tax law require. Deleting your account does not cancel a subscription: cancel it in your App Store or Google Play settings.
  • Copies others made. If you shared a list and someone added a copy to their own map, that copy belongs to them.
