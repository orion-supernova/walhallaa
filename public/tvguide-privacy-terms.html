Privacy Policy

This privacy policy applies to the TV Guide app (hereby referred to as "Application") for mobile devices that was created by Murat Can Koç (hereby referred to as "Service Provider"). The Application is offered free of charge, with no ads and no in-app purchases, and is provided "AS IS".

Summary

The Application does not collect, store or share any personal data. There are no accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking of any kind.

What the Application Stores On Your Device

The following is saved on your device only and is never sent to the Service Provider or anyone else:

  • Your favourite channels.
  • Your programme reminders. These are local notifications scheduled by your device's operating system; no push notification service or server is involved.
  • Your custom tabs (saved filters) and settings, such as the selected country, language and layout.

If you uninstall the Application, this data is removed with it.

Network Requests

To show TV listings, the Application downloads public, read-only files:

  • TV listings are downloaded from GitHub Pages (orion-supernova.github.io), operated by GitHub, Inc. Like any web server, GitHub may process your IP address in order to deliver these files. See GitHub's privacy statement.
  • Channel logos and programme images are loaded from the public image servers of the broadcasters and listing providers.

No identifier, account or personal information is sent with these requests. The Application never asks which country you are in through location services: the guide country is taken from your device's region setting, or chosen by you in Settings.

The Application does not collect:

  • Your name, email address, phone number or postal address.
  • Your physical location.
  • Your contacts, photos, microphone or camera data.
  • Your advertising identifier (IDFA). The Application never asks for App Tracking Transparency permission.
  • Any analytics or crash-reporting events. The Application contains no analytics or advertising SDK.

Third Party Access

The Service Provider does not sell, rent or share any information, because it does not collect any. The only third parties involved are GitHub (hosting the listing files) and the broadcasters' image servers, as described above, and Apple, which distributes the Application through the App Store under its own privacy policy.

Children

The Application collects no data from anyone, including children under the age of 13.

Changes

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time. Any changes will be published on this page. This privacy policy is effective as of 2026-11-13.
