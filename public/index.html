    About

    One developer, the whole app.

    I'm Murat, an iOS and macOS developer who likes to own the whole product. I take an idea from a rough sketch to a polished release, and I stay with it long after launch day.

    Ten of my apps are live on the App Store and more than ten of my projects are open source, from a clipboard manager for the Mac to a real-time party game that runs on iPhone, Android and the web. Swift and SwiftUI are my home ground, and I reach for Flutter or React Native when an app has to be everywhere at once. The things I care about most are sync that survives a bad connection, data that stays on your device, and interfaces that feel calm and fast.
