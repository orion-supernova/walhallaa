    About

    One developer, the whole app.

    A clipboard manager, a catalogue of what a phone knows about itself, a watch-party tool for the Mac, a real-time party game, a speed reader. Designed, written, submitted and maintained by me.

    Swift and SwiftUI are where I'm quickest, and where most of this lives. When a project has to land on iOS, Android and the browser at the same time, I reach for Flutter instead — the party game and the reader are both Dart. What keeps my attention lately: real-time sync that survives a bad connection, keeping data on the device, and interfaces that don't shout.
