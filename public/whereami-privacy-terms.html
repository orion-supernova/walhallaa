Privacy Policy

This privacy policy applies to the Where am I? app (hereby referred to as "Application") for mobile devices that was created by Murat Can Koç (hereby referred to as "Service Provider"). The Application is offered as a free download with optional one-time in-app purchases for additional location packs. The Application is provided "AS IS".

What the Application Stores On Your Device

To let you create and rejoin rooms without an account, the Application stores the following on your device only:

  • A randomly generated identifier (UUID v4) created the first time you launch the app. This is the only piece of "identity" the app uses. It is never linked to your name, email, phone, or any other personal data.
  • The display name (codename) you typed for yourself.
  • Your selected interface language.

If you uninstall the app, this data is removed with it. The random identifier regenerates the next time you install — your previous display name and any rooms you were in cannot be re-associated with you.

Information Sent to the Backend

While playing online, the Application sends the following to the Service Provider's backend (Convex) so a room can be shared between players:

  • The random per-install identifier described above (used as a bearer token to authenticate your requests).
  • The display name you typed.
  • The room code, room state, and game configuration (number of spies, round duration, etc.).
  • Push notification tokens (Firebase Cloud Messaging on Android; Apple Push Notification service on iOS) used to deliver round-end notifications and Live Activity updates. These tokens are device-scoped and not linked to any personal account.
  • Your interface language, so the server can localize push notification text.
  • Standard request metadata (timestamps, IP address) is logged transiently by the backend for rate-limiting and abuse prevention.

The Application does not collect:

  • Your real name, email address, phone number, or postal address.
  • Your physical location.
  • Your contacts, calendar, photos, microphone, or camera data.
  • Any third-party advertising identifiers (IDFA on iOS, AAID on Android).
  • Any analytics events. The Application contains no analytics SDK (no Google Analytics, no Firebase Analytics, no Mixpanel, etc.).

In-App Purchases

The Application offers optional one-time, non-consumable in-app purchases for additional location packs (e.g. Istanbul Pack, Cyberpunk Pack). Purchase processing is handled by Apple (App Store) on iOS or Google (Google Play) on Android. The Service Provider does not see, store, or have access to your payment card or billing details — that information stays with Apple or Google.

Purchase validation and entitlement tracking is handled by RevenueCat (RevenueCat, Inc.), a third-party service. RevenueCat receives the following:

  • The same random per-install identifier described above (used as the "App User ID" for purchase tracking).
  • The platform-issued purchase receipt (provided by Apple or Google).
  • The product identifier of the bundle you bought.

RevenueCat does not receive your name, email, or any other personal information. Their privacy policy is available at https://www.revenuecat.com/privacy.

Third Party Access

The following third parties are involved in delivering the Application's functionality:

  • Convex — hosts the real-time backend that synchronizes the game state between players. Receives the data described in "Information Sent to the Backend" above.
  • Apple App Store / Google Play Store — process payments, deliver purchase receipts, and distribute the app. Their own privacy policies apply.
  • RevenueCat — validates purchase receipts and tracks entitlements. Receives anonymous identifiers and receipts only.
  • Firebase Cloud Messaging (Google) — delivers push notifications on Android. Receives the push token and the notification payload.
  • Apple Push Notification service — delivers Live Activity updates and push notifications on iOS. Receives the push token and the notification payload.

The Service Provider may disclose User Provided and Automatically Collected Information:

  • as required by law, such as to comply with a subpoena or similar legal process;
  • when they believe in good faith that disclosure is necessary to protect their rights, protect your safety or the safety of others, investigate fraud, or respond to a government request;
  • with their trusted services providers who work on their behalf, do not have an independent use of the information disclosed to them, and have agreed to adhere to the rules set forth in this privacy statement.

The Application does not sell, rent, or share information with any party for advertising or marketing purposes.

Opt-Out Rights

You can stop all collection of information by the Application by uninstalling it. Use the standard uninstall process for your device or app store. Once uninstalled, the per-install identifier is gone and the server cannot re-associate any future installation with your prior data.

Data Retention Policy

Game state (rooms, players, in-flight rounds) is automatically deleted by the backend within hours after the room becomes inactive. Push tokens are deleted when revoked by the OS or after a period of inactivity. If you would like the Service Provider to delete any data associated with your random identifier, please contact muratcankoc@gmail.com with the identifier (you can find it by emailing us; we do not display it in the app) and they will respond in a reasonable time.
