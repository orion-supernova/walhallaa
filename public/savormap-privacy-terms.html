Privacy Policy

This privacy policy applies to the SavorMap app (hereby referred to as "Application") for mobile devices, created by Murat Can Koç, trading as WalhallaApps (hereby referred to as "Service Provider"). SavorMap turns the places you save on Instagram, TikTok, Google Maps and elsewhere into one map. It is free to use, with an optional subscription, SavorMap Pro. It contains no ads and no analytics SDK.

Your Account

You sign in with Apple or with Google. From that sign-in the Service Provider receives your account identifier and, if you share them, your name and email address. There are no passwords. Your account is stored on the Service Provider's backend (Convex) so your map syncs across your devices.

What You Save

The Application stores what you choose to save, so it can show it on your map:

  • Links you share or paste (for example an Instagram reel, a TikTok or a Google Maps place), and the post's public caption, creator handle and thumbnail where the platform makes them available.
  • The places found in them: names, addresses, neighborhoods, cities, coordinates, categories, short tags and notes such as dishes or items the post recommends.
  • Lists you create, their emoji, color and cover photo, and the share link if you share a list.
  • Screenshots you choose to save (SavorMap Pro) and photos you pick as list covers. These are stored in Convex file storage.
  • Files you import from other apps (Instagram, TikTok and Google data exports, or .kml / .geojson / .csv place files). Only the saved posts and places in them are kept.
  • Simple usage counters needed to run the service, such as how many AI imports you used this month.

Clipboard and Location

When you open the Application it can check whether you copied a link, so it can offer to save it. On iOS you confirm with Apple's Paste button; on Android, copied Instagram, TikTok or Google Maps links are saved automatically, and you can turn this off in Profile. Nothing from your clipboard leaves your device unless it is a link you save.

If you allow it, the Application uses your location to show you on the map and to sort nearby places. Your location is used on your device only. It is not sent to or stored by the Service Provider.

How Places Are Found

To turn a saved post into places, the Application sends data to these services:

  • Google Gemini API (Google) receives the post's caption, or the screenshot you saved, and returns the places it names. The Service Provider uses the paid Gemini API, under which Google does not use this content to train its models.
  • OpenStreetMap Nominatim receives place names and addresses (not your identity) to find their coordinates.
  • Instagram (Meta) and TikTok receive the link you saved, so the Application can read the post's public caption and thumbnail.

Subscriptions

SavorMap Pro is sold through Apple (App Store) and Google (Google Play). The Service Provider never sees your payment details. Purchase status is handled by RevenueCat (RevenueCat, Inc.), which receives your SavorMap account identifier as its "App User ID", the store's purchase receipt and the product you bought. RevenueCat's privacy policy is at https://www.revenuecat.com/privacy.

Shared Lists

If you share a list, anyone with its link can see the list's title and the names, areas and map locations of its places, on the web or in the app, and can add a copy to their own map. If the list allows friends to join, people who join see each other's added places in that list. Your other saves stay private. You can stop sharing at any time.

Third Party Access

  • Convex hosts the backend and database (in the EU) and stores the data described above.
  • Apple and Google provide sign-in, payments, app distribution and the maps you see (Apple MapKit on iOS, Google Maps SDK on Android). Their own privacy policies apply.
  • Google Gemini API, OpenStreetMap Nominatim, Instagram and TikTok, as described in "How Places Are Found".
  • RevenueCat, as described in "Subscriptions".

The Service Provider does not sell, rent or share your information for advertising or marketing. Information may be disclosed when required by law, or to protect the rights and safety of users or the Service Provider.

Data Retention and Deletion

Your data is kept until you delete it. You can remove any save or list in the Application, and you can delete your whole account in Profile → Delete account, which erases your saves, places, lists, screenshots, photos and account. See Delete your account for details, including how to ask by email. Purchase records are kept by Apple, Google and RevenueCat as their policies and tax law require.

Children

The Application is not directed at children under 13, and the Service Provider does not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child has provided personal information, contact info@walhallaa.com and it will be deleted.

Security

All traffic between the Application and its backend uses HTTPS. Sign-in tokens are stored in the device's secure storage.

Free Use and SavorMap Pro

SavorMap is free to use. Free accounts keep every saved place and include 30 AI imports per calendar month; after that, saves still work using a simpler, non-AI reader until the next month.

SavorMap Pro is an auto-renewing subscription, offered monthly or yearly, that adds unlimited AI imports, saving places from screenshots, lists friends can join and add to, and list customization (cover photos, emoji and pin colors). Prices are shown in the Application before you buy and may vary by country.

  • Payment is charged to your Apple ID or Google account when you confirm the purchase.
  • The subscription renews automatically unless you turn off auto-renew at least 24 hours before the end of the current period. Your account is charged for renewal within 24 hours before the period ends.
  • You can manage or cancel the subscription in your App Store or Google Play account settings at any time. Cancelling stops future renewals; Pro stays active until the end of the period you paid for.
  • Refunds are handled by Apple or Google under their refund policies. The Service Provider cannot issue refunds for store purchases.
  • Use "Restore purchases" in the Application to restore Pro on a new device.
