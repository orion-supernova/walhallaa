Privacy Policy

This privacy policy applies to the NestZone app (hereby referred to as "Application") for mobile devices that was created by Murat Can Koç (hereby referred to as "Service Provider"). NestZone helps the people who share a home keep their chores, shopping, notes, messages, recipes, movie lists, plans, money and house problems in one place. It is free to download, with an optional subscription, NestZone Plus.

Summary

NestZone stores what you and your household put into it, so that everyone in the house sees the same thing. It is shared only with the members of your household. There is no advertising, no analytics SDK, no tracking across apps and websites, and your data is never sold.

Your Account

You sign in with Apple. From that, the Service Provider receives an identifier that is unique to NestZone and, the first time only, the name and email address you chose to share. If you used "Hide My Email", the address is a private relay address from Apple. You can change your name and profile photo in the app.

What Your Household Shares

A household is a private space that people join with an invite code. Everything added to it is visible to its members, and only to them:

  • Chores and who finished them, shopping lists, notes, messages and polls.
  • Recipes, meal plans, movie lists and movie-night votes.
  • Calendar events, house problems and their photos.
  • Shared expenses, bills, budgets and settle-ups. These are amounts you type in; NestZone never connects to a bank or card.
  • Your name and profile photo.

Photos and Camera

Photos you choose (a profile photo, a photo of a house problem, a recipe or a message) are uploaded so your household can see them. The photo library is opened through Apple's photo picker, which gives the Application only the pictures you pick. The camera opens only when you tap to take a photo.

Notifications

If you allow notifications, your device's push token and the app version are stored so that the household's updates reach you. Notifications are delivered through Apple Push Notification service.

Purchases

NestZone Plus is sold through the App Store. Apple handles payment; the Service Provider never sees your card. Subscription status is managed by RevenueCat, Inc., which receives your purchase receipts and your NestZone user identifier in order to tell the Application whether your household has NestZone Plus. See RevenueCat's privacy policy.

Where Data Is Kept

Household data is stored on servers operated by the Service Provider. Movie information and posters come from The Movie Database (TMDb); your searches go through the Service Provider's server, so TMDb receives no information about you. This product uses the TMDb API but is not endorsed or certified by TMDb.

What the Application Does Not Collect

  • Your location, contacts, microphone or health data.
  • Your advertising identifier (IDFA). The Application never asks for App Tracking Transparency permission.
  • Analytics or advertising events. The Application contains no analytics or advertising SDK.

How Long Data Is Kept

Household content stays until someone in the household deletes it, or the household is deleted. The household activity feed is cleared after 90 days. When the last member leaves a household, the household and its content are deleted.

Deleting Your Account

You can delete your account at any time in the Application: Settings → Delete account. This deletes your profile, your photo, your devices and your sign-in, removes you from every household, and deletes any household you were the last member of. Things you added to a household that others still use, such as a message or a chore, stay for them without your name. Deleting your account does not cancel a subscription; cancel it in your Apple ID settings.

Children

The Application is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect their personal information. A parent who believes a child has created an account can contact the Service Provider to have it deleted.
